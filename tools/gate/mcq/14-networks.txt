?1 [Layering and switching] Which layer of the TCP/IP model is responsible for process-to-process delivery?
(a) link layer
(b) network layer
(c)* transport layer
(d) application layer
=> The network layer delivers packets between hosts, and the transport layer uses port numbers to deliver data to a particular process on the host.

?1 [Layering and switching] In the TCP/IP model, the unit of data handled at the transport layer by TCP is called a
(a) frame
(b) packet
(c)* segment
(d) bit stream
=> TCP data units are segments, IP data units are packets (datagrams), and link-layer units are frames.

(a) 12 µs

!py assert close(1500 * 8 / 100e6 * 1e6, num(ans.split()[0]))

?2 [Layering and switching] A message of 12 000 bits is sent over 3 store-and-forward links in series, each of rate 1 Mbps. Ignoring propagation and queuing delays, the message is split into 3 equal packets of 4000 bits. The time until the destination has received the whole message is
(a) 12 ms
(b)* 20 ms
(c) 24 ms
(d) 36 ms
=> Each packet takes 4 ms on a link. With pipelining the last packet leaves the source after 3 packet times and then crosses the remaining 2 links: (3 + 3 − 1) × 4 ms = 20 ms. (Sent as a single packet it would take 3 × 12 = 36 ms.)
!py assert num(ans.split()[0]) == (3 + 3 - 1) * 4
